BLUEPRINT / BP-04
28 migrations deployed. The full BP-04 system is live.

All 656 properties, 656 buildings, 26,888 units, 1,452 business profiles, 1,490 organisations, 1,477 organisation sites and 43,412 employment dossiers are present in the verified live snapshot. Property and premises context is connected to organisation dossiers, workforce, agreements, assets, infrastructure, incidents, dispatch, scenes and case review.

Validation: 29/29 BP-04 tests passed; the full gameplay regression passed 97/97. A rollback-only live test also created a temporary active officer, executed a property-event command through the real runtime and verified actor attribution and command audit completion. The officer and all test records were rolled back.
