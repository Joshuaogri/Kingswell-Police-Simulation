# Kingswell Neighbourhood Atlas — BP-04 / KNG-MAP-0.2

**Setting:** Kingswell, capital of the fictional Republic of Asterfell.  
**City role:** Primate city — national political, financial, commercial, entertainment, logistics, transport and international-trade centre.  
**Map:** Fictional local grid, 20,000 m east–west × 16,000 m north–south; north-up; metres.  
**Coordinate system:** KNG-MAP-0.1. **Property-map layer:** KNG-MAP-0.2.  
**Fiction:** All properties, organisations, businesses, ownership records, values and map geometry are fictional game data.

This atlas extends the existing BP-02 district and neighbourhood records. It does not create a second map hierarchy, new streets or duplicate property identities. Every mapped place resolves to the canonical `game.properties`, `game.buildings`, `game.building_units`, `game.property_addresses` and `game.property_access_points` records.

## The city at a glance

Kingswell is the country's dominant urban centre. Its national role is visible in its skyline, institutional core, financial district, wholesale markets, industrial estates, airport, central passenger interchange and southern harbour. It is also a place where people live: dense residential towers, campus housing, neighbourhood shops, cafés, clinics, childcare, repair services and riverfront mixed-use premises connect the major economic districts to everyday life.

## District and neighbourhood guide

The district is intended to feel inhabited throughout the day rather than empty outside office hours.

## Property record and map detail

Every property has a linked cadastral/title profile, zoning and permitted-use data, valuation fields in fictional Asterfell currency, utility connections, security, fire-safety and accessibility records, a parcel outline, map label and planning notes. Every building has a structural/architectural profile, floor directory, height estimate, building systems and footprint outline. Every unit has a floor/suite/use classification, internal area, occupancy capacity, utility-meter references, access notes, security zone, condition and description.

Ownership and occupation are distinct. Each property has a current registered-owner record; landlord and property-manager interests are separately recorded. Each operating business has an organisation identity, business profile and current site record linked to a specific unit. The site record distinguishes tenant from owner-occupier and can hold lease references and terms.

## Text-first map and property commands

The game exposes read-only commands:
- `property summary by PERSONNEL_REF`
- `property map KDW-01 by PERSONNEL_REF`
- `property record KNG-P-RH0001 by PERSONNEL_REF`
- `business directory by PERSONNEL_REF`
- `business directory sector logistics_warehousing by PERSONNEL_REF`
- `business directory district KLG by PERSONNEL_REF`
- `business directory search "Kingswell" by PERSONNEL_REF`
- `business record AF-BIZ-000281 by PERSONNEL_REF`

The neighbourhood map query returns all properties in that neighbourhood, each parcel's local-grid centre and outline, address, road, access points, building footprints, floor count, unit totals and current occupiers. The citywide summary returns district totals, land-use mix and business-sector counts. All these commands are read-only and do not advance fictional time.

## Spatial and realism boundaries

- Local-grid property and building outlines are schematic fictional geometry, not surveyed cadastral polygons or architectural plans.
- Each property has one primary access point in the current seed. Secondary entrances, internal floor plans, turn restrictions and service-lane geometry remain later refinements.
- New properties reuse existing neighbourhoods, named roads, road segments and junctions. No second geography hierarchy has been created.
- Business employee totals are estimates attached to business profiles. Actual employees, directors, owners, customers and suppliers are persistent NPCs only when their linked population/employment systems seed or promote them.
- No live traffic, market prices, actual import manifests, real-world addresses or real-world companies are implied.

## Game interfaces and ownership boundaries

BP-02 owns the city and road geography. BP-03 owns person identities, households, population and employment links. BP-04 owns properties, buildings, units, addresses, access points, property interests, business profiles and organisation site/occupancy records. BP-05 and later police divisions use these canonical places for incidents, enquiries, access checks, CCTV requests and business/person links. No later division should recreate a property, building, business or address identity.
